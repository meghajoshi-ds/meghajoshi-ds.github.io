AML Transaction Monitoring

Four detection rules combined with an Isolation Forest on 60,059 synthetic transactions. False positives fell from 5,245 to 89 and all 139 planted laundering cases were still caught. I then tested what happens when a launderer changes behaviour to dodge the rules.

Machine learning

Credit Risk Scorecard

Logistic regression and LightGBM models predicting loan default on 163,987 Lending Club loans. I found that interest rate leaks the answer, removed it, then validated on a later year of loans. Ranking held up, but predicted default rates came out 6 to 7 points too low, so the model would need recalibrating before use.
